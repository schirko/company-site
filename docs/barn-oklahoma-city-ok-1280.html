Best Months to Sell

Calves and heavy feeders run opposite seasons: calves usually bring the least in the fall run and the most in spring; heavy feeders the most in late summer. The typical year is measured on 5 markets' USDA reports since 2021, with the market's overall rise taken out; barns don't differ beyond the year-to-year noise, so each barn shows the region's pattern for the months it sells.

Steer calves, 500-600 lb

Steer calves here typically bring the most in Mar (about +5% vs an average month) and the least in Nov (about -6%). This season's latest month, Oct 2026, ran -6%; a typical Oct runs -3%.

Feeder steers, 800-900 lb

Feeder steers here typically bring the most in Jul (about +5% vs an average month) and the least in May (about -4%). That gap is within the year-to-year noise, so treat it as a lean, not a rule. This season's latest month, Oct 2026, ran +1%; a typical Oct runs +2%.

Where the Numbers Come From

Herd Planner's price model: the hub market (Oklahoma City) plus each barn's own measured difference from it; the range is where 8 in 10 real sales fell when the model was tested on sales it never saw. This barn is the hub: its prices come straight from the model fitted on its own sales. Medium and large frame, muscle grade 1 cattle.
