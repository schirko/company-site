Where the Number Comes From

A day counts as workable when it had under 2.5 mm of rain and under 12.5 mm over the two days before, the same rule the Equipment Planner uses to price harvest delays. We count those days from October 1 to November 30 in every fall since 2000: the typical fall is the median, the wet fall the 10th percentile. It counts rain only, not snow or frozen ground.
