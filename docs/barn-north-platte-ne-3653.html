Best Months to Sell

Calves and heavy feeders run opposite seasons: calves usually bring the least in the fall run and the most in spring; heavy feeders the most in late summer. The typical year is measured on 5 markets' USDA reports since 2021, with the market's overall rise taken out; barns don't differ beyond the year-to-year noise, so each barn shows the region's pattern for the months it sells.

Steer calves, 500-600 lb

Steer calves here typically bring the most in Mar (about +5% vs an average month) and the least in Nov (about -6%). This season's latest month, Sep 2026, ran -9%.

Where the Numbers Come From

Herd Planner's price model: the hub market (Oklahoma City) plus each barn's own measured difference from it; the range is where 8 in 10 real sales fell when the model was tested on sales it never saw. Medium and large frame, muscle grade 1 cattle.

Source: USDA AMS MyMarketNews auction reports. Estimates, not quotes: check with the barn before you sell. How we test Herd Planner.
