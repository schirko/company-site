Where the Number Comes From

A straight-line trend through the county's USDA corn yields since 2000 says what a normal year looks like now. The low end is the 10th percentile of each year's yield relative to its trend: 1 year in 10 does worse. It's the county's recent normal and its downside, not a forecast of this season.
