Privacy

The short version, for all of our apps.

  • We never sell or rent your email address or your records, and never use them to target ads.
  • Your records are yours. Herd Planner lets you download your whole herd as a spreadsheet at any time, and you can ask us to close your account.
  • Email only with your permission. We send news only if you tick the box asking for it, and every email has a way to stop.
  • Public data is public. Prices, yields and weather come from USDA and NASA; your own records are never shared with other users.
  • Results you choose to share. If you share a field's harvest in My Farm, we keep only the summary you confirm (county, crop, year, acres, yield, moisture, how sure you are, and a field name if you give one). An uploaded file is read and deleted. We use them to test and improve the apps, never sell them, never show one farm's numbers to anyone, and publish only averages across at least 5 farms. You can delete them in My Farm any time.
  • Your fields and yield maps. Field outlines you add in My Farm, and a field's yield map if you tick the box to keep it (yields in 12 m squares, never the single readings), and the yields you type into a field's history are seen only by you. They're never shared, sold or shown on this site, and Delete removes them for good. To show a field's soils, its outline (with no name or account) is sent to USDA's Soil Data Access, the public soil survey service, and to show what grew there each season, to USDA's Cropland Data Layer image service.
  • Requests from sale barns. If you run a sale barn and send Update This Page, we keep what you send (your name, role, how to reach you, an email if you give one, the notice and the corrections) so we can call your office to check it and keep a record of what was posted. Only the notice is shown, on your barn's page, until the day you chose. Each barn page asks My Farm for its notice when it opens; that request carries no cookie and nothing about the visitor.
  • Following a sale barn. If you follow a barn (Follow This Barn on its page, or Your Barns in My Farm), My Farm keeps which barns you follow and whether, and when, you asked for their notices by email. Its notices show on your My Farm home page, and by email only if you ticked that box. The barn never sees who follows it: we never give your name or email to a barn. Every email has a Stop These link for that barn alone, and Your Barns changes any of it at any time. A barn's page asks My Farm whether you follow it, the same way the menu at the top asks whether you're signed in.
  • Barn page views. When a barn's page opens, it asks My Farm for the barn's notice. We count those asks per barn per day so the barn can see how many times its page was opened. Only the number is kept: nothing about you, your browser or where you are. A barn sees its own count and how many people follow it, never who.
  • Barn sale calendars and reminders. A barn's page asks My Farm for the sale calendar the barn keeps there, the same way it asks for its notice; nothing about you is sent or kept. If a barn asks for a reminder of one of its sales, it goes by email only to people who follow that barn and ticked the box for its notices, a couple of days before the sale, with the same Stop These link.

Each app has its own full terms and privacy notice, which you agree to when you sign up. Those are the ones that apply:

  • Herd Planner: terms of use and privacy.
  • Yield Predictor: no account and nothing personal collected; its disclaimer is on every page.
  • Grazing Planner: no account and nothing personal collected; its disclaimer is on every page.
  • Farm Equipment Planner: no account and nothing personal collected; its disclaimer is on every page.

This website itself sets no cookies and runs no tracking or advertising scripts. It keeps two small notes in your own browser, which are never sent to us: the county and sale barn you pick on the home page, and, if you come here from one of our apps while signed in, which apps those were (not who you are), so the menu can offer to open an app in place of “Sign In” and can take you through that app, which checks whether you’re still signed in.

When a page opens, it asks My Farm (where you sign in to every app) whether this browser is signed in there, so the top of the page can show a menu under your first name (My Farm, Sign Out) instead of Sign In. My Farm answers only to this website, with yes or no and your first name, never your email; it already knows who you are, and nothing new is kept.

To reach us, use the Tell us button in Herd Planner. It comes straight to us.
