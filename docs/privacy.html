Privacy

The short version, for all of our apps.

  • We never sell or rent your email address or your records, and never use them to target ads.
  • Your records are yours. Herd Planner lets you download your whole herd as a spreadsheet at any time, and you can ask us to close your account.
  • Email only with your permission. We send news only if you tick the box asking for it, and every email has a way to stop.
  • Public data is public. Prices, yields and weather come from USDA and NASA; your own records are never shared with other users.

Each app has its own full terms and privacy notice, which you agree to when you sign up. Those are the ones that apply:

  • Herd Planner: terms of use and privacy.
  • Yield Predictor: no account and nothing personal collected; its disclaimer is on every page.
  • Grazing Planner: no account and nothing personal collected; its disclaimer is on every page.
  • Farm Equipment Planner: no account and nothing personal collected; its disclaimer is on every page.

This website itself sets no cookies and runs no tracking or advertising scripts. It keeps two small notes in your own browser, which are never sent to us: the county and sale barn you pick on the home page, and, if you come here from one of our apps while signed in, which app that was (not who you are), so the menu can offer to open that app in place of “Sign In”.

To reach us, use the Tell us button in Herd Planner. It comes straight to us.
