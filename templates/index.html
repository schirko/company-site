How we build them

Your records stay yours

We never sell or rent your email address or your records, and never use them to target ads. Herd Planner lets you download your whole herd whenever you like.

Public data, checked

Prices, yields and weather come from USDA and NASA. Every source is named, and every model is tested on data it hasn't seen before we trust it.

Honest ranges

Markets and weather are uncertain, so our answers come with a range, not just a single number, and the reasons behind them.

Works where you work

Built for a phone at the chute or the edge of a field, in plain words, with no training needed.
